# Checking against simulating

Generated by `make bench`. The ratio is the time to simulate a claim's conclusion to tolerance 1/1000 (or the bound's own tolerance) with failure probability 1/1000, using Hoeffding's sample count, over the wall-clock time of `axiom check` on the whole certificate. A ratio above one means checking is cheaper. The hypothesis "checking is much cheaper" is confirmed for a kind when the ratio is at least 10 on every fixture with a claim of that kind, and refuted otherwise. Times marked `*` were extrapolated linearly from a prefix of the samples. Ratios marked `>=` are lower bounds, because no finite simulation covers a whole parameter range.
